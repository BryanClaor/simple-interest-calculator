# Simple Interest Calculator

## Overview

The Simple Interest Calculator is a basic tool that calculates the
interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specified period
using a fixed annual interest rate.

## Formula

Simple Interest is calculated using this formula:

**Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100**

Where: - **Principal** --- the initial amount of money. - **Rate** ---
the annual interest rate in percent. - **Time** --- the duration in
years.

## Example

Suppose the principal is ₱10,000, the annual interest rate is 5%, and
the time is 2 years.

Simple Interest = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = ₱1,000

**Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest = ₱11,000**

## Purpose

This project demonstrates the basic calculation of simple interest and
helps users understand how principal, interest rate, and time affect the
interest amount.

## Inputs

-   Principal amount
-   Annual interest rate (percentage)
-   Time period (in years)

## Outputs

-   Simple interest
-   Total amount (principal plus interest)

## Notes

This calculator uses simple interest, not compound interest. The result
is an estimate and does not include fees, taxes, or other charges.
